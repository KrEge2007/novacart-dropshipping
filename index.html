Our story

A catalog that stays small on purpose

Most fitness shops list ten thousand products and hope you'll dig. We do the opposite: each week we review what's available from our suppliers and publish a short edit of gear worth owning — for strength, mobility, and the slow work of recovery.

When the week ends, the catalog rotates. What stays is the standard: simple tools, honest prices, and nothing we wouldn't use ourselves.

Questions, answered

How does shipping work?

Orders ship directly from our supplier warehouses. Standard delivery is 5–9 business days with tracking, free on orders over $60.

Why does the catalog change every week?

We publish a small edit rather than an endless catalog. Every Monday the selection rotates based on what's in stock and worth recommending. If you see something you like, don't wait too long.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Photograph it and reply to your order confirmation within 30 days — we'll replace it or refund you in full, no return postage needed.

Where are you based?

We're a small team in Norway. Products ship from supplier warehouses in Europe, the US, and Asia depending on stock.
